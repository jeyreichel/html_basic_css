This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the following:

SELF-STORAGE OWNERS BUSINESSOWNERS COVERAGE FORM

Subparagraph 4. Loss Payment-Building and Personal Property of Paragraph E. LOSS CONDITIONS in Section I – Special Property Coverage Form is amended to include the following:

We will pay the difference between the actual replacement cost and the amount that would have been paid under this policy if you are prohibited from repairing, rebuilding or replacing the Covered Property to the same like kind and quality, height, floor area, number of units, configuration or occupancy due to restrictions imposed by any applicable ordinance or law that regulates construction zoning or use, provided however that such loss is not otherwise recoverable.

This endorsement applies only to Covered Property insured on a replacement cost basis.

The amount payable under this endorsement is part of, and not in addition to, the applicable Limits of Insurance.

All other terms and conditions remain unchanged.
