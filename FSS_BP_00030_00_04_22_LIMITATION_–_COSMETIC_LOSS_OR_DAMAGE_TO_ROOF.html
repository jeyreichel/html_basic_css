LIMITATION – COSMETIC LOSS OR
DAMAGE TO ROOF

This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the following:

SELF-STORAGE OWNERS BUSINESSOWNERS COVERAGE FORM

SCHEDULE*

Designated Location(s):

*Information required to complete this Schedule, if not shown above, will be shown in the Declarations.
  1. The following provision is added to Subparagraph 5. Limitations of Paragraph A. COVERAGE under SECTION I – SPECIAL PROPERTY COVERAGE FORM:
    1. For the Building(s) at the Location(s) designated in the SCHEDULE of this endorsement, we will not pay for "cosmetic loss or damage" to the "roof covering" of such Building(s) if such "cosmetic loss or damage" is caused by hail, wind, rain, snow or ice.
  2. For the purposes of this endorsement, the following definitions are added to Paragraph G. Property Definitions of SECTION I – SPECIAL PROPERTY COVERAGE FORM:
    • "Cosmetic loss or damage" means that damage to any portion of the "roof covering" of a building that alters the physical appearance of the "roof covering", but does not result in damage that allows the penetration of water through the "roof covering" or does not result in the failure of the "roof covering" to perform its intended function to keep out the elements over an extended period of time.
    • "Roof covering" means:
      1. The roofing material exposed to the elements;
      2. The underlayments applied for moisture protection; and
      3. Decking, roof doors, roof vents, roof turbines, and all strippings, flashings and edging required in the replacement of the roofing material or underlayments.

All other terms and conditions remain unchanged.
